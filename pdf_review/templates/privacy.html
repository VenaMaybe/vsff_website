PDF Review is a private beta operated by Vena for sharing PDFs and collecting feedback, primarily from beta readers and advanced copy readers. It is a work in progress. This policy explains how information is handled when you use PDF Review.

Information we collect and why

  • Account information. Google sign-in provides your name, email address, email verification status and a unique Google account identifier. We store these along with your account ID, sign-in dates and chosen display name and review color to identify you, manage beta access and attribute your feedback.
  • Documents and feedback. We store uploaded PDFs, filenames, document titles, version information, sections, selected text and highlight locations, comments, replies and their revision history to provide document review and preserve context.
  • Reading activity. Tracking is on by default. When you view a PDF, we record pages visited, active viewing time, visit dates, last and furthest positions, and estimated completion. Document owners can see this activity for readers with current access; other readers can see only their own analytics. Owners can turn tracking off for the document or pause it for individual readers in Review settings. Pausing or turning tracking off retains earlier history. Completion is an estimate.
  • Presence and preferences. We process live connection and current page/spread information to show who is viewing a version. Live presence is temporary; separately recorded reading activity is retained. We save review and display preferences and access-management records to operate the service.
  • Technical information. Hosting and security infrastructure may process IP addresses, browser information, request times and error logs to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service. Information you email to us is used to respond to your request.

Google sign-in

We request only basic sign-in permissions: openid, email and profile. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or calendars, and do not receive your Google password. We do not store Google access, refresh or ID tokens after sign-in or fetch your Google profile photo. Google handles authentication under its privacy policy.

Who can see information

Documents are available to accounts granted access by their owners. Feedback and display names are visible according to the document's Shared or Isolated review settings; owners can see all feedback. Document owners can see beta members' names and email addresses in the sharing directory to choose collaborators. Site administrators manage account and beta access information; site administration alone does not grant access to document content.

Vena and service providers supporting hosting, network delivery, security and backups may process information as needed to operate and support PDF Review. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to address abuse or protect people's rights and security. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. The app does not include advertising trackers or third-party marketing analytics.

Cookies and browser storage

We use a session cookie for sign-in and security. Your browser also remembers tool layouts, display choices and derived PDF statistics such as word and image counts. The statistics cache does not store PDF files, extracted page text, comments or reading history. Browser storage may remain after logout; you can clear it through your browser's site-data controls. Clearing browser data does not delete information stored on the server such as PDFs or comments.

Retention, security and your choices

Beta data is retained while needed to provide the service, preserve review history, maintain backups and address security or legal needs. There is currently no automatic deletion schedule. Deleting a version removes it from normal access but retains its PDF and historical records internally. Deleting a comment hides its current body while retaining revision history. Removing beta access does not erase the account or its contributions.

We use access controls and protected connections in production. Collaborators may retain copies they already received. Keep your own originals and share only information with collaborators you trust.

You can update your display name and review color in Account. For access, correction, account closure or deletion requests, contact Vena at yppahhappy.com@gmail.com. We review requests manually, verify identity where needed and explain any information that must be retained, including shared review history or backups, subject to applicable law. To pause reading tracking, ask the document owner. You may also remove PDF Review's Google connection in your Google account settings; doing so does not delete existing PDF Review data.

Changes and contact

We will update this page as the service changes and provide notice in the app or by email of material changes before they take effect. Questions about this policy can be sent to yppahhappy.com@gmail.com.
